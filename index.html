Questions

The things people ask us most.

Is Stride really free?
Yes. The Free plan has no time limit. You only pay if you want Plus or Yearly.
What kind of goals can I set?
Anything you can turn into a habit: exercise, reading, studying, sleep, saving money and more.
What happens if I miss a day?
Nothing bad. Your coach adjusts the plan and helps you get back on track the next day.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. You can cancel from your settings in one click.
